A local partner. A global perspective.

Softtex Fashions Wear Limited is a 100% export-oriented garments manufacturer and buying house based in Dhaka, Bangladesh.

Who we are

Softtex Fashions Wear Ltd. has bought and sourced apparel in Bangladesh since 2018. We are the buying bridge between buyers and importers on one side, and manufacturers and exporters of readymade garments on the other.

We work closely with renowned Bangladeshi garment manufacturers. Every order is followed strictly, with quality assurance, standard inspections and on-time delivery.

Our global merchandising team serves buyers worldwide for finished apparel, textiles and readymade garment stock lots. Competitive prices, good quality and on-time delivery are our main strength.

Our Mission

We strive for the greatest possible satisfaction and mutual benefit for our customers and manufacturers, with a constant focus on quality, on-time delivery and the best possible value for money.

What we make

We manufacture and export baby clothes, kids’ clothes, women’s and men’s clothing, T-shirts, polo shirts, hoodies, sweatshirts, pants, shorts, skirts, dresses, sportswear, school uniforms, sports uniforms, corporate uniforms and custom clothing, in knitwear, denim and woven fabrics.
