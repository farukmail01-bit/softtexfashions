Our Process

From First Brief to Final Shipment

A structured and transparent process to make your sourcing journey smoother and more efficient.

01

Understand Your Brief

We discuss your product requirements, target market and specifications.

02

Source the Right Partners

We identify and work with suitable factories based on your needs.

03

Develop & Approve Samples

04

Monitor Quality & Shipment

We follow up production, conduct quality checks and coordinate shipment to your destination.

About Softtex

A local partner. A global perspective.

SOFTTEX FASHIONS WEAR LIMITED is a 100% export-oriented garments manufacturer and buying house based in Bangladesh. We work with international fashion brands, retailers and wholesalers to source a wide range of apparel through a reliable network of manufacturers. Our focus is on building long-term relationships through transparency, professionalism and a commitment to your business goals.
